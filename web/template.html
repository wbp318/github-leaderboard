GitHub Contribution Tornado

Open source on GitHub: fix a placement, add a feature, star it ★

The tornado

Each ring is a place (or, for one state, a person), the busiest on top. Its red half is public commits; its blue half is every other contribution, including private ones for people who show them on their profile. Dots are the people orbiting it; yours is gold. Click a ring to go down a level.

Leaderboards

Ranked by category, for the whole country, a region, a division, a state, or a Louisiana parish. Your row is gold; if you are outside the top 100, your row is added at the end.

The states

Click a state on the map or in the table to open its tornado. Map colour: total contributions of the people counted. Dimmed states are not counted yet. Find yourself above and "Your rank there" shows where you would place among the 80 people compared in every state.

Regions and divisions

Louisiana, parish by parish

All 64 parishes. People are placed by the town, city, university, or parish named in their GitHub location; those who wrote only "Louisiana" are under "Parish not given". Louisiana was also searched by city name, so it is covered more deeply than other states.

Built with no frameworks: one HTML file, a canvas, and GitHub's API. The code, the placement rules, and the daily refresh are at github.com/wbp318/github-leaderboard. Someone in the wrong state? Open an issue or add a test case and send a pull request.
