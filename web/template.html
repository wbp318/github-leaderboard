Storm Cellar

The tornado shook the bugs loose. A little first-person shooter in the old blocky style, drawn and coded for this page: clear out the cellar, find the five commits and the neighbours sheltering there, and get to the exit, with Biscuit the dog at your heels. Then go down the storm drain under it, where something big is waiting by the way out.

Controller connected: its buttons are under Keys. Mouse up and down
Keys

Change any key: click Change, then press the new one. A key another action uses swaps over. Enter, Escape, and Tab stay as they are. Kept in this browser.

ActionKeys

Controller

No controller yet. Plug one in (an Xbox controller works, by USB or Bluetooth) and press any button on it.

ActionButtons

Click the game or press Enter to play. W A S D or the arrow keys move, the mouse or the left and right arrows turn, click or Space shoots, the mouse up and down looks up and down (or, with V or the switch below, walks you forward and back), 1 and 2, Q, or the mouse wheel switch weapons, Shift runs, M turns the sound off and on, and Esc pauses. Walk up to a neighbour sheltering from the storm and they'll tell you what they know, and Biscuit, Hank's dog, follows you and goes for the bugs. Paused, S saves your progress in this browser, and C on the title picks it up again; the buttons below do the same. On a phone, drag the left pad to move and turn, hold Fire, and tap Swap or the gun at the bottom left to switch weapons.

The tornado

Each ring is a place (or, for one state, a person), the busiest on top. Its red half is public commits; its blue half is every other contribution, including private ones for people who show them on their profile. Dots are the people orbiting it; yours is gold. Click a ring to go down a level.

    The states

    Click a state on the map or in the table to open its tornado. Map colour: total contributions of the people counted. Dimmed states are not counted yet. Find yourself above and "Your rank there" shows where you would place among the 80 people compared in every state.

    Every county

    Every county in the 50 states, coloured by the people counted there. Click a state to zoom in, then a county to see its leaderboard. GitHub's search finds about 800 people in each state, so this is a sample: a blank county means no one has been found there yet, not that no one codes there.

    Louisiana, parish by parish

    All 64 parishes. People are placed by the town, city, university, or parish named in their GitHub location; those who wrote only "Louisiana" are under "Parish not given". Louisiana was also searched by city name, so it is covered more deeply than other states.

    Built with no frameworks: one HTML file, a canvas, and GitHub's API. The code, the placement rules, and the daily refresh are at github.com/wbp318/github-leaderboard. Someone in the wrong state? Open an issue or add a test case and send a pull request.
